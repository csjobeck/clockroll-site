Clockroll

Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Clockroll collects no data. Your location, drives and work hours are processed and stored only on your iPhone. I never receive them, and there is no account, server, analytics or advertising.

Who is responsible

Clockroll is made by Christian Sjøbeck, Denmark. Contact: christian@sjobeckdigital.dk.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Location. With your permission, iOS tells Clockroll when you arrive at or leave the places you added (home, workplaces, sites) and when it detects a visit. Clockroll doesn't follow your route with GPS.
  • Bluetooth beacons. iOS tells Clockroll when you're near a beacon you added, for example in your car.
  • Motion. If you allow Motion & Fitness, Clockroll checks whether time between two places was spent in a vehicle, to suggest drives you made without your beacon.
  • Your entries. Work sessions, drives, notes and settings are stored in the app on your iPhone. If your iPhone backup is on, they're part of that backup, under your control.

Tracking only during work hours

If you choose to track only during your work hours, events outside them are thrown away on your iPhone straight away and never saved. To start and end drives and sessions correctly, the app remembers only whether you're currently inside each of your places.

Sharing

Nothing leaves your iPhone unless you export a timesheet and choose where to send it.

Apple services

Address search uses Apple Maps, and purchases are handled by Apple. I don't receive your searches or your payment details.

Children

Clockroll is not directed at children and doesn't collect any information from anyone.

Your rights

Because nothing is sent to me, there is nothing for me to export or delete. Delete entries in the app, or delete the app to remove everything. Under the GDPR you can contact me with any questions, and you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
