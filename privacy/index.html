Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Your location, drives and work hours are processed and stored only on your iPhone. I never receive them. The only thing Clockroll sends me is anonymous usage data about which features are used, which you can turn off, and which never includes places, times, hours or drives.

Who is responsible

Clockroll is made by Christian Sjøbeck, Denmark. Contact: christian@sjobeckdigital.dk.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Location. With your permission, iOS tells Clockroll when you arrive at or leave the places you added (home, workplaces, sites) and when it detects a visit. Clockroll doesn't follow your route with GPS.
  • Bluetooth beacons. iOS tells Clockroll when you're near a beacon you added, for example in your car.
  • Motion. If you allow Motion & Fitness, Clockroll checks whether time between two places was spent in a vehicle, to suggest drives you made without your beacon.
  • Your entries. Work sessions, drives, notes and settings are stored in the app on your iPhone. If your iPhone backup is on, they're part of that backup, under your control.

Tracking only during work hours

If you choose to track only during your work hours, events outside them are thrown away on your iPhone straight away and never saved. To start and end drives and sessions correctly, the app remembers only whether you're currently inside each of your places.

Sharing

Your hours, places and drives never leave your iPhone unless you export a timesheet and choose where to send it.

Anonymous usage data

To find out which features are used, Clockroll sends anonymous usage signals through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in Germany that processes data on my behalf.

Signals are sent only when you do something in the app, never from tracking in the background. They record that something happened, with coarse details. For example:

  • setup was finished, with whether tracking is limited to work hours and the number of places in broad ranges such as “2–5”
  • a place or beacon was added, with its type (home, workplace, site, car)
  • an entry was confirmed as work or marked personal in Review
  • a timesheet was exported, with its format
  • the Pro screen was shown, or Pro was bought

Each signal also includes general technical details such as the app version, iOS version, device model and language. Signals never contain locations, place names, addresses, arrival or departure times, hours, drives, notes or anything else you enter.

Instead of an identifier that could identify you, TelemetryDeck receives a hashed, anonymous identifier, so I can count how many people use a feature but can't tell who they are. The data isn't linked to you, isn't used for advertising, and isn't combined with data from other apps or websites. TelemetryDeck doesn't sell or share it.

To turn it off, open Clockroll, tap Settings, then turn off Share anonymous usage. Nothing is sent after that.

Apple services

Address search uses Apple Maps, and purchases are handled by Apple. I don't receive your searches or your payment details.

Children

Clockroll is not directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect any information from them.

Your rights

Your hours and places are only on your iPhone: delete entries in the app, or delete the app to remove everything. The usage data is anonymous, so I can't find, export or delete it for a particular person; switching off Share anonymous usage stops it immediately. Under the GDPR you can contact me with any questions, and you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
