Automatic, your way

  • Places: home, your workplace, customers and job sites. Arrivals and departures are noticed even when the app is closed.
  • Car beacon: a small Bluetooth beacon (iBeacon) in the car or at your desk starts and stops the clock.
  • Your car's Bluetooth or CarPlay: a guided Shortcuts automation logs drives, no beacon needed.

Private by design

Choose to track only during your work hours: outside them nothing is recorded. No account, no cloud, no ads. Your hours and places stay on your iPhone.

You stay in charge

Everything automatic waits in Review: Work or Personal, one tap. Add or correct anything by hand. Export daily totals, every entry, or a PDF timesheet for your employer.
